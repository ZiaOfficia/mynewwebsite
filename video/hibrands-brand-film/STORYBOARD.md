# HiBrands intro film: storyboard

**Concept:** "When the world makes premium growth feel out of reach, HiBrands brings it within reach."
A dark, cinematic film where cold barriers of glass break into a connected world of light,
told entirely in on-screen words so it works muted on the website.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, 45.6 s (19 bars at 100 BPM; every scene starts on a bar line).
**Palette:** midnight (#05070F to #0B1024), white type, electric blue (#3050F0), violet (#7B5CFF),
cyan (#1EC8F0), and HiBrands marigold (#FFC83D) as the warm gold. The final lockup uses the
official logo colours: marigold bubble, cobalt "Hi".
**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque (display) and Geist (body), the website's fonts.

**Rules:** no prices, no invented statistics, testimonials or customer logos; the real logo is never
distorted; every line holds long enough to read at phone size; nothing important sits outside the
title-safe area (90% of frame).
