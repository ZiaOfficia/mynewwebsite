Open with the problem in plain words. Who is this for, and what will they be able to do after reading?

## First main point

Short paragraphs. Use real examples from client work where you can.

## Second main point

- Lists are fine for steps or checklists
- Keep each item to one idea

### A smaller heading

You can add images: put the file in `public/blog/` and write
![Describe the image](/blog/your-image.jpg)

## What to do next

End with one clear next step for the reader.
