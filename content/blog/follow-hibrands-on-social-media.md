## Why we are on social media

When we started HiBrands, we promised to be the opposite of a big agency. You talk directly to the engineers who build your website and run your campaigns. Nobody passes you between departments.

Social media is the natural next step of that promise. A website tells you what we offer. Our social pages let you **see how we actually work**, ask a question in a comment or a message, and get an answer from a real person, not a sales script.

> A business you can message, comment on and watch at work is a business you can trust.

## What you will find on each platform

Each platform is good at something different, so we use each one a little differently.

### Instagram: the work, up close

On [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hibrands.in/) we share the visual side of what we do: websites and landing pages as they launch, before-and-after designs, and short Reels with tips you can use the same day. It is also the quickest place to send us a direct message.

### Facebook: for local business owners

Many of the business owners we work with already use [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595383445932) every day. There we post practical advice in plain language, announce new offers and answer questions in the comments.

### X: quick updates and ideas

On [X](https://x.com/hibrands_) we post short updates: changes on Google that affect local businesses, quick SEO and ads tips, and news from HiBrands as it happens.

### LinkedIn: for professionals and partners

Our [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hi-brands-9a1913444/) is where we talk about the business side of digital growth. If you run a company, work in marketing or would like to partner with us, this is the place to connect.

### YouTube: learn it step by step

Some things are easier to show than to write. On [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@hibrandss) we post walkthroughs and explainers, like how to set up a Google Business Profile, what makes a landing page convert, and how to read your own website's numbers.

## How following us helps your business

Following a marketing team might sound like it only helps us. Here is what you get out of it:

- **Free, practical tips.** The same advice we give paying clients, in short posts you can act on yourself.
- **Real examples.** See the websites and landing pages we build for businesses like yours, so you know what is possible before you spend anything.
- **Offers first.** New prices and limited offers are announced on our pages before anywhere else. You can see our current prices on the [web development](/services/web-development#pricing) and [landing page](/services/landing-pages#pricing) pages.
- **A direct line to us.** A message on any platform reaches the people who do the work. No call centre, no ticket number.
- **A clear picture of who we are.** You can see our work, our style and how we talk to people long before you decide to work with us.
